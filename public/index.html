            Frequently Asked Questions

            Everything you need to know about ResumeMatch AI and our screening algorithms.

            Is ResumeMatch AI free?
            +
            Yes. You can run an initial resume analysis completely free of charge to get your overall ATS compatibility score, format check, and basic skill recommendations. Upgrades are available for unlimited analyses, custom job matching, and full career roadmaps.
            What file formats are supported?
            +
            We support PDF (.pdf), Microsoft Word documents (.docx), plain text (.txt), and image screenshots (.png, .jpg, .jpeg). Image uploads are parsed using optical character recognition (OCR) before screening.
            Can I paste a custom job description?
            +
            Yes. You can choose between calibrated company profiles (Google, Amazon, TCS, Zomato, etc.) or switch to "Custom JD" mode to paste any job requisition directly from LinkedIn, Indeed, or company careers pages.
            How is the ATS score calculated?
            +
            Our scoring engine evaluates four core dimensions: Role Alignment (semantic matching against the job description), Skill Match (technical competencies and tool proficiencies), Requirements Coverage (experience and hard requirements), and Company Calibration (formatting health, section completeness, and hiring standards).
            Does ResumeMatch AI guarantee interviews?
            +
            No. ResumeMatch AI is an optimization and diagnostic guide engineered to dramatically improve your resume's ATS pass rate and highlight blind spots. We do not guarantee employment, as final hiring decisions depend entirely on employer evaluations and interview rounds.
